About Me

Hi, I'm Lucas — an Economics major at Penn State, graduating in May 2027. This summer I've been working as a sales intern at ADP's Allentown, Pennsylvania office, where our team serves a client base that is entirely Canadian. Yes, you read that right: an office in eastern Pennsylvania whose customers are all north of the border. That one detail has taught me more about how modern business actually works than any single class.

On the sales floor, I've worked through the full cycle — prospecting, discovery calls, objection handling, and follow-up — for HR and payroll solutions. What surprised me most is how much of sales is really just applied economics: understanding incentives, reading what a business actually values, and figuring out where the friction is.

In the classroom, my coursework in money and banking has covered the Fed, interest rates, inflation, and fixed-income markets — topics that stopped feeling theoretical the moment I started talking to real business owners about real budgets.

Outside of work and school, I'm usually watching Philadelphia sports, playing golf, or hanging out with my two dogs, Charlie and Archie. I'm still deciding exactly where my career goes after graduation — sales, finance, or somewhere the two overlap — and this site is where I think out loud about what I'm learning along the way.
