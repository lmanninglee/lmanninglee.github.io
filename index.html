About Me

My name is Lucas. I am pursuing Economics degree at the Pennsylvania State University and will graduate in May 2027. Over the course of this summer, I have been interning at ADP in the Allentown, Pennsylvania office where the entire client base is located in Canada. Indeed, an office in Pennsylvania whose client base is in Canada. It is one fact that has made me learn much more about the business world than any of my classes.

On the sales floor, I have gone through the entire cycle including prospecting, discovery call, handling objections, and follow-up calls for HR and payroll solutions. The biggest surprise has been how many sales is all about economics - knowing the incentives, understanding the value of business, and where the friction lies.

In my classroom, I have studied money and banking with all the concepts revolving around Fed, interest rate, inflation, and fixed income markets which lost their abstract nature the moment I started speaking to businesses.

Outside of work and school, I'm usually watching Philadelphia sports, playing golf, or hanging out with my two dogs, Charlie and Archie. I'm still deciding exactly where my career goes after graduation, sales, finance, or somewhere the two overlap, and this site is where I think out loud about what I'm learning along the way.
